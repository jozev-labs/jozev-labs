Virtual fencing dashboard

Live demo, simulated data

A browser simulation of a ranch: 48 collared cows, 8 pastures, 3 radio gateways. A fence shrinks to move the herd to the next field. Then things break. A gateway goes offline, GPS (Global Positioning System) signals lie, and a fence update never reaches 8 collars.

It started as an unpaid interview exercise for a cattle startup. My job was the database design. Two decisions were mine. Every table carries an organization id, so one platform can serve many ranchers. And a fence move goes through three states: planned, pushed, executed. For the exercise I also learned to read Bluetooth Low Energy and LoRa (long-range radio) messages and tested them on a couple of real devices I own.

The brief called a move done when 80% of collars confirmed. I think that's too low, because the other 20% are cows standing in the wrong field. So the demo judges each collar and shows the move as partial.

The company changed focus before I could show them any of it, so I rebuilt it as a simulation. I designed it and directed it. Claude wrote the code.

JozEV electric moped

Prototype stage, Ride JozEV B.V.

A premium electric moped in the L1e class, the EU category for light mopeds (the Dutch bromfiets). Steel frame, naked-bike look, throttle, lights, motor, controller and battery. The first goal is a working prototype that meets the minimum rules for road registration.

It's my company, so this is the project where I'm the engineer, the product owner and the one fixing the wiring.

WingDash

In progress

A GPS cockpit display for a Segway E110 electric moped. It runs on an ESP32 (a small microcontroller with WiFi and Bluetooth), a touchscreen and a GPS module.

Running WiFi, Bluetooth, radar and the map together used up the memory, so I cut it down to two modes: a WiFi setup portal and a ride screen. The setup portal and the radar screen work today. The map view is being rebuilt.
