Why we built this

Card payments weren't designed for your table

Most payment tools are built for shops with a fixed till — not for someone setting up a table for a weekend and packing it away Sunday night.

€

Fixed fees crush small tickets

A flat per-swipe fee sounds small until you're selling €3 stickers all day — it can eat your entire margin on your smallest, most popular items.

⏳

Onboarding built for companies

Most processors expect a registered business. Independent artists selling under their own name shouldn't need paperwork they don't have.

📉

"Intro rates" that climb later

A low rate to get you signed up, then a jump once you're relying on it. We'd rather tell you the real number up front.

How it works

From sign-up to sold out, in four steps

No business registration, no dashboard to babysit — just a table, a phone, and your art.

1

Sign up in minutes

Verify your identity once — name, address, ID, and IBAN. No company registration required.

2

Get set up

Install the Jozev-Pay app and pair your card reader. Ready before doors open.

3

Sell at your table

Accept tap, chip, and contactless. Customers get an instant digital receipt.

4

Get paid out

Your sales land straight in your IBAN account — no separate payout app, no waiting on a dashboard.
